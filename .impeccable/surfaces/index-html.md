# Surface brief: falamos.app home (index.html)

Scope: the public home page of the Falamos app. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: people moving to / just moved to Portugal; Ukrainian speakers first, English-speaking expats too.
Job: understand in seconds what Falamos is (European Portuguese for real relocation) and know it is coming to App Store
and Google Play. Action: store plates, «coming soon» until live (no fake links). No prices or free-tier limits.
Proof/content: store screenshot copy (en/uk), the night-tram header video, the real lines of the course.
Constraints: EN default, `#uk` switch like privacy/; privacy/ and config.json untouched; no invented reviews or numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a tram stop. The lines leaving it are Finanças, renting, the doctor and A2 — not «features». Refuses
the split hero with a phone mockup and a row of three icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Eléctrico from the app. Tram-yellow ground by day, warm graphite by night; ink route-board signs with
yellow lettering; white boards with 2px ink edges and hard 6–8px offset shadows; metro line colours (#2B63C6, #C2381F,
#17845A, #7A3FC2) as square line numbers; Geologica for interface text, Bricolage Grotesque only on Portuguese.

STORY: A newcomer sees the stop sign (Falamos, European Portuguese for people who moved), reads the departures board
and finds their own worry on it; rides on: the night tram passes, each line opens into a stop that shows the app's
answer (the letter explained, the dialogue, pt-PT not Brazilian, a practice item in the CIPLE format; first release is all of A1, A2 next); ends at the terminus with the
store plates again and support.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a full-height ink pole on the left carries the stop sign (logo bubble, «Falamos», tagline, two
«coming soon» store plates, language switch). Right, a departures board ~60% wide: header «Lines from this stop»,
four rows, each a coloured line number, the Portuguese destination in Bricolage and the worry in Geologica. Phone: sign
on top, board below, the pole as a continuous left rail behind both, on the axis of the stops' rail.

FORM: Paragem — the stop board; dealt lead (position 7 on my ordered list of 7). seed:ffad3694e07e02ed

Signature interaction: the board rows are links that ride down to their stop; the night-tram video plays as the
section between the board and the stops (paused under reduced motion, poster only, with a pause control). Motion: the tram video is the page's one moving
moment; the board flip was dropped on review (2026-11-15) because a whole-word tilt read as a glitch.

Questions before you get on

Is it European or Brazilian Portuguese?

Only European Portuguese (pt-PT), as it is spoken in Portugal: chamo-me, not me chamo; autocarro, not ônibus; telemóvel, not celular. A Brazilian form in the course is treated as a mistake and fixed.

Who is Falamos for?

For people who are moving to Portugal or already live there and need the language for real life: the tax office and the NIF, renting a home, the doctor, the bank, school.

Will it help me with the CIPLE A2 exam?

A2 is the level needed for permanent residence and citizenship. The course goes level by level towards it, and each level ends with a practice test in the CIPLE format: reading, listening, writing and speaking. Both A1 and A2 are in the app.

Can it explain a letter from Finanças or AIMA?

Yes. Photograph a letter, a contract or a sign: the text is recognised on your phone, and only that text is sent to be explained in your language. Pick the words you need, and they join your review.

Which languages are the explanations in?

English and Ukrainian. The Portuguese is the same for everyone; translations and grammar notes are written for your language.

Do I need an account?

No. Falamos works without registration. You can sign in with Apple or Google to keep your progress when you change phones.

Where can I download it?

Falamos for iPhone is in the App Store. The Android version is coming soon to Google Play.
